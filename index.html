Drop‑in setup
Point your merchant at the facilitator and you’re done.
  1. 1.Set FACILITATOR_URL= in your server.
  2. 2.Choose a network:
    solana, solana-devnet, base, base-sepolia,
    polygon, polygon-amoy, avalanche, avalanche-fuji,
    sei, sei-testnet, peaq, iotex.
  3. 3.Ship. The facilitator verifies & settles payments on your behalf.

Features

Multi‑network
Solana‑first, with EVM support (Base, Avalanche, Sei, IoTeX).
Point one endpoint at many chains. The facilitator abstracts network quirks so your server doesn’t have to.
Any token
ERC‑20 on EVM; SPL & Token‑2022 on Solana.
Use stablecoins or your own tokens. Configure per‑endpoint pricing in fiat or atomic units.
Gasless experience
No blockchain fees for buyers or merchants.
The facilitator covers network fees and handles verification/settlement so you can focus on your product.
1‑second start
No API keys. Just plug & play.
Start with a single env var and standard endpoints (/verify, /settle, /list).
Consistent API
One simple interface across networks.
You get reliability, observability, and reuse across multiple merchants and apps.
Built for scale
Designed for human & agentic use‑cases.
From pay‑per‑call APIs to AI agents, settle payments in under a second when the chain confirms.
